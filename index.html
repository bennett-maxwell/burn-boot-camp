Franchise Opportunity Profile

Burn Boot Camp

Group fitness boot camp franchise: focused group workouts, nutritional and goal-setting assistance, and the Burn App virtual platform. Every fact on this page is sourced from the Burn Boot Camp 2025 Franchise Disclosure Document (issued April 30, 2025), with Item and page citations.

Key Facts

Initial Franchise Fee

$60,000

Nonrefundable, uniformly imposed. 15% VetFran discount for qualifying veterans. (FDD Item 5, p.6)

Estimated Initial Investment

$281,899 to $645,344

Single franchised business. Area development (2-3 units): $326,899 to $715,344. (FDD Item 7, p.16; cover)

Royalty

6% of Gross Revenues

Per Report Period, payable by direct deposit. (FDD Item 6, p.9)

System Brand Fund

2% of Gross Revenues

May increase up to 3% on 90 days' notice. (FDD Item 6, p.9; note 3, p.14)

Local Advertising

$15,000 + $15,000 + $2,500/mo

$15,000 pre-opening, $15,000 grand-opening window, $2,500/month thereafter. (FDD Item 6, note 3, p.14)

Technology Fee

$860 per month

Cap $1,500/month. $100/month until lease signing, then $860/month. (FDD Item 6, p.9; note 5, p.14)

Territory

Protected (not exclusive)

3-mile radius, or the radius yielding a 50,000 population; broad franchisor reserved rights. (FDD Item 12, pp.44-46)

Area Development

2-Pack / 3-Pack

Development fees: $105,000 (2-pack) / $130,000 (3-pack), due at ADA signing. (FDD Item 5, p.8)

Term

5 years + two 5-year renewals

First renewal $0; second renewal $10,000. (FDD Item 17, p.56; Item 6, p.11)

Facility

3,500 to 7,000 sq ft

Shopping centers, malls, or free-standing buildings. (FDD Item 7, note 5, p.17)

HQ

Cornelius, North Carolina

17036 Kenton Dr., Suite 100, Cornelius, NC 28031. 1-833-289-2876. (FDD Item 1, p.1)

System Size

365 outlets

356 franchised + 9 company-owned at end of 2024; net +21 in 2024. (FDD Item 20, Table 1, p.70)

Risk Profile

33 / 100 risk score

Assessment: Lower-moderate. A large, still-growing system (299 to 365 outlets, 2022–2024) with broad Item 19 coverage and audited financials, tempered by six 2024 franchisee terminations, a 6%+2% ongoing fee load, and a protected-but-not-exclusive territory.

  • Capital intensity: investment ceiling $645,344 — pushes score up
  • Ongoing fee load: 6% royalty + 2% brand fund = 8% — pushes score up
  • Territory: protected, not exclusive; broad franchisor reserved rights — pushes score up
  • Terminations: 6 franchisee terminations in 2024 — pushes score up
  • Item 19 coverage: 278 of 365 outlets (76.2%) reported — pushes score down
  • Unit growth: net +21 outlets in 2024 — pushes score down
  • Financials: audited statements attached as Exhibit G — pushes score down
